# GTM Notes

## Stage
- **D2C:** generally available, minimal marketing so far — Phase I → early Phase II (validating; no formal launch yet).
- **Institutional:** Phase I — actively recruiting **10–15 institutions for Fall 2026 pilots**. First customer: **Hinds Community College** (all students, this Fall).
- Strategic constraint: **no mass-scale paid acquisition before the product is validated.**

## The two motions (review each on its own terms)

| | D2C (primary) | Institutional (growing) |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | The student (user = buyer) | Success center / advising / Canvas admin; leadership sponsors budget |
| Message | Results, no wasted effort, walk in ready | Safe, sanctioned, secure, bounded cost, learning science |
| CTA | Start 7-day free trial | Join the Fall 2026 pilot |
| Proof needs | Beats the free stack at a moment that matters | Security, LMS integration, integrity story, a partner to call |

## Pricing (public)
- **Individual:** $5/month after a 7-day free trial. Sign-up at **studywithathena.com**; payments via Stripe / Apple App Store.
- **Institution-sponsored:** free to the student; school pays. Students verify through Canvas. Instructure acts as a FERPA-designated school official under sponsored agreements.
- **Don't:** invent discounts, annual pricing, or per-seat rates (none published); publish internal adoption numbers; promise dashboards, admin controls, or dates.

## Funnel reality (D2C)
- The audience defaults to free tools; the funnel must **demonstrate the Canvas-connected difference before asking for payment**. The "watch — it just knows" moment is the activation hook.
- Willingness to pay is the riskiest assumption. High-stakes moments (exam crunch) are when the upgrade is earned.
- CAC ceilings are tight at $5/mo — model payback before recommending any paid channel.
- ~Half of current users don't connect Canvas — the connection moment is a critical funnel step, not a given.

## Channel strategy (Bullseye framework — run separately per motion)
- **Likely A-list (untested hypotheses):** existing platforms (the Canvas/LMS ecosystem we already sit inside) · sales + BD (institutional pilots via success centers, advising, Canvas admins) · community/offline events (single-campus density tests) · engineering-as-marketing (free study tools that beat the free stack).
- **Channel test log:** none logged yet — recommendations should propose cheap, time-boxed tests (~$1k / few weeks) that answer: cost per customer, volume available, right customers?
- Testing ≠ optimizing: first prove a channel works at all.

## Launch completeness expectations
- Enablement already exists and must stay in sync: public FAQ, CSM FAQ, sales/AE internal FAQ, outreach emails.
- Institutional artifacts respect the register split: security/privacy/integration first.
- Boilerplate requires Instructure comms sign-off before external use.

## Review guidance for directors
- Walk the full path per motion: first touch → trial/pilot → Canvas connection → activation ("it just knows") → paid/renewal. Flag broken handoffs, especially payment asks before demonstrated value.
- Tie recommendations to a traction goal and one honest metric per funnel stage; flag vanity metrics.
- Size advice to stage: this is validation-stage GTM, not scale-stage — cheap tests over big campaigns.
- New launches should update the existing FAQ/enablement set; flag staleness as a finding.
