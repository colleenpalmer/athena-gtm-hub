# Brand

## Masterbrand idea
**"The tutor that knows you best."** A principled coach — not a spoon-feeding answer machine, not a stodgy professor. The category stance: an independent, learner-owned, **LMS-native AI study coach** on the "learn, don't shortcut" side of the category.

## Archetype & personality
**Warm expert coach** — smart, approachable, calm, credible; a step ahead but never showing off. You're relieved to have them because you never doubt their competence. Voice pillars: approachable · authentic · **"humble brightness."**

- **We are:** calm, crafted utility · supportive companion warmth · grown-up delight · anti-slop.
- **We are not:** peer study-buddy, stodgy professor, enterprise LMS software, commodity cheat-tool energy, generic AI aesthetics, "unlock your potential" hype.

## Emotional territory
- **Dominant note: capability / earned progress** — the student leaves *more capable* and holding the credit. Transformation, not "I got help."
- **First-contact arc (priority order):** relief → clarity → quiet "it just worked" delight. Relief must be earned by real progress — hollow reassurance is the "AI slop" failure mode.
- **Cardinal never-feel: judged / made to feel dumb.** Secondary: **confused** (the inverse of the orientation promise). Make them do the work; never make them feel small.

## Brand architecture (locked decisions)
- **One brand = product *and* coach.** The incumbent persona is fully retired; no separate character name, no learner-configured persona. The student is the hero; the brand is the steady guide in their ear.
- Consumer-grade identity: downloaded, recommended, remembered on its own terms — never software students are "assigned."

## Relationship to Canvas — stand apart, leverage the house
- Canvas is the **hero feature and campaign fuel, not the identity frame**. 
- Canvas can be connected to by a student **if a school allows it**
- Institutions can sponsor students' monthly payment to Athena

## Expression quality bar
- **Solid > clever.** Copy and creative reinforce what the product delivers; they don't carry it alone.
- **AI is the engine, never the badge.**
- Brand expression peaks at first touch and emotional moments; recedes into calm utility during study tasks.
- Celebrations: specific, earned, tied to demonstrated mastery — never attendance theater.

## Review guidance for directors
- Run the competitor test against **both** camps: generic AI chatbots (ChatGPT) and study apps (Quizlet). If either could ship the artifact unchanged, it's not distinctive enough.
- Watch for drift toward the three failure modes: study-buddy (celebrating attendance, cutesy), professor (pedagogy lectures at students), and answer-machine (shortcut energy).
- Growth constraint: must stretch down to a 16-year-old later without feeling collegiate today.
- **The gut-test:** would a student feel solid — not cheap, not embarrassed — recommending this to a friend?
- For visual identity specifics (logo, color, type), consult the `athena-rebrand` and `brand-boards` repos; treat undocumented visual decisions as foundation gaps to flag.
