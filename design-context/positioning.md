# Positioning

## Positioning statement
For **undergraduates who genuinely want to learn the material** and **can't tell where their gaps are**, **Athena** is a **personal AI study coach** that **knows your actual Canvas courses and knows you — so it tells you the right thing to work on next and coaches you until you truly understand it.** Unlike **general chatbots and generic study apps**, we **connect securely to your real course content, track what you've mastered, and coach you to the answer instead of handing it over.**

- **One-liner:** A personal learning coach that knows your actual classes *and* knows you — the fastest honest path to doing better in the courses you care about.
- **Strategic promise:** "Do better in the classes that matter — because you actually get it, not just get past it."
- **Category:** independent, learner-owned, LMS-native **AI study coach** — reframing the category away from answer engines toward coached mastery.

## Messaging pillars

1. **Knows your class (Canvas-native, zero setup):** reads real courses, materials, deadlines, exams automatically — nothing to upload. Surfaced as **Study Spaces**. The "watch — it just knows" demo moment.
2. **Knows you (no wasted effort):** tracks mastery and gaps — "you've nailed three, let's drill the two weak ones." Walk in ready.
3. **Coaches, doesn't cheat (real understanding):** guides you to the answer instead of handing it over; won't produce submission-ready work.
4. **Safe and sanctioned (institutional register):** the safest place to study with AI is a vetted tool built by the company that runs your LMS. Read-only, revocable, no training of external models.

## Differentiation (why us)

1. **The precision intersection — knows your class *and* knows you.** No competitor combines LMS-native course context with a rigorous personal mastery model.
2. **Only study tutor that connects directly and securely to Canvas** — zero-friction context, institutional trust, and a data moat competitors can't replicate.
3. **Principled coach-don't-tell** — the most *efficient* path to real learning and results.

## Competitive alternatives & angles

| Alternative | Their strength | Our angle |
|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT (free) | Free, ubiquitous, habitual | Hands over answers you don't retain; no course context; no gap tracking. "A month later, do you still understand it — or did you just copy it?" |
| Quizlet (free) | Familiar, big set library | Generic and manual. "Is your study set built from *your* lecture notes — or someone else's?" |
| Upload-your-syllabus AI tools | Some personalization | Manual upload friction exactly when stress is highest; no live LMS connection. |
| Do nothing (cram) | Zero cost, zero effort | The persistent "am I missing something?" — orientation is the cure. |

**Biggest risk:** price vs. free habit. Willingness to pay is the riskiest assumption — needs a high-stakes hook or the free institutional path.

## Approved proof points (do not invent others)

- 90% of higher-ed students already use AI in class at least occasionally (Instructure research).
- Hinds Community College: first institutional customer, offering Athena to all students this Fall.
- Read-only Canvas OAuth2; credentials encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM); disconnecting revokes access.
- No training of external models on Athena data — contractually barred for every AI provider.
- **Known gap:** no published efficacy stats or named student testimonials yet — flag claims needing them as "needs proof."

## Register split (load-bearing)
Students hear **results + "no wasted effort"**; institutions hear **the learning science and safeguards that produce them**. Same truth, two registers — never both at equal weight to one listener.

## Review guidance for directors
- Every artifact must survive "why not free ChatGPT?" — the differentiated mechanism is Canvas-native context + mastery tracking + coaching. Flag generic "study smarter" claims as category wallpaper.
- The claim test: "helps you study" is wallpaper; "knows your actual courses" is ours.
- Check unsupported claims against the proof-point list; the correct finding is "needs proof", never a suggested stat.
- Institutional messaging must stay consistent with the learner-owned, student-authorized framing.
