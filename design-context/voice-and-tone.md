# Voice & Tone

## Voice in a sentence
Athena is a **warm expert coach** — the brilliant person who makes *you* feel smart: calm, credible, and steadily confident at the 11pm-before-an-exam moment ("I've got you, I know the drill, I'll guide you through this"). Explicitly NOT the peer study-buddy, NOT a stodgy professor.

## We are / we are not

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Approachable | Cold or clinical |
| Authentic | Hype-y or salesy ("more marketing, less tool") |
| "Humble brightness" — clever, not show-offy | Know-it-all / condescending |
| Clear, confident, conversational | Jargon-heavy or wordy |
| Warm and supportive | Saccharine, cutesy, or "cringe" |
| Calm and credible | Urgent, shaming, or nagging |
| Solid > clever | Trend-chasing wordplay |

## Tone by context

- **Website / product:** calm, crafted utility — competent and frictionless before flashy. "More tool, less marketing."
- **Sales / enablement (institutional):** credible and reassuring; lead with security, privacy, integration, partnership. This register is where learning science and "powered by IgniteAI" live.
- **Social:** friendly, calm, supportive — meet students at the stuck moment so it reads as relief, not a pitch. Hooks may lean urgent when the platform demands; body stays clear and confident.
- **Support / lifecycle email:** a coach checking in — warm, personal, a little celebratory when earned; never a company "retaining a subscriber."
- **Exec / analyst / PR:** confident and principled; explain the student-authorized model; never apologize or overclaim.

## Style rules

- Talk to the student as "you"; active voice; short sentences; bullets over walls of text.
- **Lead with the benefit/results** for students; save mechanism and learning science for the institutional register.
- Reading level: accessible to an 18–22-year-old; must stretch down toward high school without feeling collegiate.
- **AI is the engine, never the badge** — don't lead with "AI-powered" in student-facing copy.
- **Celebration discipline — "applaud mastery, not attendance":** every celebration must be specific, earned, and tied to demonstrated competence, never activity or logins.
- **Delight calibration:** mostly quiet "it just worked" smoothness, light sprinkle of personality. When they conflict, frictionless wins.

## Words & phrases

- **Use:** students · study coach · coach you to the answer · knows your courses / knows you · no wasted effort · walk in ready · Study Spaces · future-ready.
- **Banned (house):** harness, unlock, leverage, empower, future-proof.
- **Avoid (playbook):** crush it, grind, actually, falling behind, as I mentioned, let's see if, that's not quite right, drill me, dominate, hustle, killing it.

## Legal / brand no-gos (blocker-level findings)

- Never say **"FERPA compliant" or "FERPA certified"** (no such certification). Correct framing: for sponsored institutions, "Instructure is designated a school official under FERPA."
- Never promise dashboards, admin controls, usage reporting, or specific dates (in design/discovery only).
- Never present Athena as part of Canvas, endorsed by a school, or required for coursework.
- "Powered by IgniteAI" — institutional register **only**.
- Never publish internal adoption numbers.

## Terminology (one name per concept)

- **Athena** — the AI study coach; masterbrand; *by Instructure*.
- **Study Spaces** — the feature name for Canvas-native course context.
- **Coach-don't-tell** — the method: guide to the answer, never submission-ready work.
- **AI study coach** — our category (not answer engine, not homework-doer).
- **Student-authorized access** — read-only OAuth to the student's own Canvas view.
- **ZPD** — institutional/team register only; don't lecture students with learning science.

## Emotional guardrails

- **FEEL:** trust, relief, encouraged, guided, safe (vulnerable, not judged), "I'm smarter because of it", clarity, progress, respected.
- **Cardinal never-feel: judged / made to feel dumb.** Secondary: **confused.** Also never: nagged, condescended to, overwhelmed, "AI slop".
- Principle: **make them do the work; never make them feel small.**

## Calibration examples

- ✅ "Okay, here's where to start." · "You've got this down — let's spend your time on the two concepts that are still shaky." · "Let's try that again."
- ❌ "You're out of time!" (shaming) · "Let's see if you got it" (tests, doesn't coach) · "That's not quite right" (deflating) · "Unlock your potential with AI-powered studying" (banned words + AI-as-badge + hype).

## The gut-test
*Would a student feel solid — not cheap, not embarrassed — recommending this to a friend?* If no, it doesn't meet the bar, however clever.
