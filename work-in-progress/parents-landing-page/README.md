# Parents Landing Page

**Status:** 🟡 In progress (positioning direction drafted, no owner or date yet)

**Hypothesis:** A landing page that gives parents of college students something worth sending to their
student can reach parents cheaply and bring in students who activate. This is a learning project first.

**Project page:** `parents-landing-page.html` (open in a browser, or http://localhost:3000/parents-landing-page/parents-landing-page.html with the app running). It is the source of truth for status, positioning, funnel, plan, and open questions.

## Direction in one line

*The AI study tool that makes your student do the work.* Primary CTA is "Send Athena to your student" (the
student starts their own trial). Secondary CTA is parent email capture. "Buy for my student" is not offered
until product confirms it's possible.
