E2

Remarketing

BacklogGo or no-go Oct 12 · tracking on Oct 16 · ads Oct 19 – Nov 9 · $1,000 limit

Show ads to people who started signing up at Hinds and quit, to bring some of them back. E1 emails students who registered and stopped. This is for people who quit before registering. We have no email for them.

Who it reaches
  • Since launch: 668 quit on the launch page and about 2,800 more quit at a later step, probably registration. None has an account, so we have no email.
  • Only people who visit from now on. The tracking only sees visitors after it's turned on (Oct 16), so the ~3,500 who already left can't be reached. How many we can reach depends on weekly arrivals (open question 11).
What we do
  • Turn on the ad platform's tracking on the launch page and registration page. It only notes that someone visited and didn't register, and only after the visitor agrees to tracking. It sends no email, student ID or school name.
  • Show ads to people who visited and didn't register, and stop once they register. Ad platforms won't run ads until enough people are in the group, so check the minimum before Oct 19.
  • Run two ads on one social platform, using the same two headlines as the flyers (A: cost, B: exam) so we can compare. Each ad links to the free sign-up route (open question 2) with ?src=remarketing.
  • Limits: $1,000 total, a cap on how often one person sees an ad, adults only.
  • Never use Hinds lists or Canvas data to decide who sees an ad. Our public FAQ says we don't.
The result will be rough. Some of these people would have come back anyway, and the group is too small to hold some people out as a comparison. E5 changes the same pages, so look at ad clicks by their own tag.

E5

Hinds sign-up path: launch page and registration page

BacklogLive Oct 16 · product builds, Hinds-launch variant

If a Hinds student hears "free for Hinds students" on both screens, and the price line and school search are gone, more of them finish registering and end up covered by Hinds.

What we change (two screens)
  • A · Launch page: today it says "You just launched Athena from your LMS." Lead with "free for Hinds students," say Canvas instead of LMS, preview the three steps.
  • B · Registration page, Hinds variant: Hinds pre-selected, no "$5/month" line, no coupon link, same right-hand panel as screen A.
Why (from the live pages)
  • Screen A never says free, names no school, and uses "LMS."
  • Screen B asks "Find my school" of a student who just came from Hinds Canvas. "Free" is conditional, and "$5/month after 7 day free trial" sits right beside it.
  • The "Create account" button sits under the school box, so a student who skips the search lands on the paid trial.
  • The two screens' right-hand panels tell different stories.
Next test, not this one: remove screen A entirely if the Canvas launch already carries the school.

E7

Student referral

BacklogPilot Oct 26 – Nov 16 · largest, needs decisions

If students already using Athena can invite classmates for a gift card, we get new students cheaply, because a classmate's word beats a flyer.

What we do
  • Email the ~300 active students a personal link. Hand-run tracking in a sheet; e-gift cards sent weekly; $500 cap.
  • Ladder: 2 classmates → $5 · 5 → $10 total · 10 → $20 total. Your $10-for-10 is cheaper, but most students never reach 10, so almost no one gets paid.
  • Counts only: a new Hinds student, via the link, who uses Athena within 14 days. Not sign-ups alone.
  • If it works, build an "Invite a classmate" button in the app.
Sizing, not a forecast: 30–90 sharers × 1–3 classmates each = 30–270 new students.

Canvas Commons package

BacklogNo date yet · starts after sign-off (open question 13) · we build, instructors import

If instructors can pull ready-made, optional Athena pages into their own Canvas course in a few clicks, more of them tell their students, and we reach schools beyond Hinds without a flyer or a Hinds email.

How Commons works (Instructure's guides)
  • Commons is where Canvas users find, import and share course pages, modules, assignments, quizzes and files.
  • Only instructors can share. A resource can go to one school's account, to groups, or public (all Canvas users). Public items may not show up if a school's admin turns that off or limits search to approved content.
  • Imported items keep their published or unpublished status, and student data stays private.
  • Updating a shared course can overwrite what an instructor changed, so share pages and modules, not a whole course.
What we do
  • One module built from the faculty kit (E4): a Start Here page, the announcement text, the syllabus line, the ungraded "try it together" activity, and a short "what Athena reads and can't do" page. Shared unpublished, so nothing reaches students until the instructor reviews and publishes it.
  • Two versions. Hinds: says Hinds covers it; given to Hinds as an export to import and share inside its own account. Public: no Hinds claims ("your school may cover Athena"), links to the main sign-up page.
  • Every link carries its own tag (?src=commons-hinds, ?src=commons). Each page says Athena is a separate study tool, not part of Canvas, and that using it is optional.
The read is rough: instructors import on their own schedule, not on Oct 19, and we don't yet know what Commons shows an author about imports. If it shows nothing, count students arriving with the tag.

Do and don't

  • "Free for Hinds students" and "Hinds covers it," once confirmed.
  • Reads your courses, can't post or submit, disconnect anytime.
  • Coaches toward the answer instead of handing it over.
  • Short, plain, phone-first.
  • Present Athena as part of Canvas, endorsed by Hinds, or required. No graded tasks.
  • Promise dashboards, reports, admin controls, or dates.
  • Imply Hinds or an instructor sees an individual student's use.
  • Collect friends' contacts, invent numbers, or use banned words (unlock, leverage, empower…).
