Before anything sends

  • D1: how do we reach them? People who left before registering have no account, so we have no email for them (same gap as E2 on the project page). Ask product: does the Canvas launch give us an email or name we keep? If yes, we can email. If no, either Hinds sends the three messages for us (a Canvas Inbox message or Hinds email to students without an account yet), or D1 doesn't run. Either way, get legal and privacy sign-off first (project page, open question 12).
  • "Free for Hinds students" isn't confirmed yet for the link we'll use (open question 2). D1 leads with it, so D1 waits on that answer. D2's copy makes no cost claim.
  • Who sends, from what address, under what consent rules (open question 5). Plain text from a named person, with an unsubscribe link and a real reply address.
  • Tagged links. D1 links use ?src=drip-new, D2 links use ?src=drip-back. Needs the tag to survive sign-up (open question 1). Without it, count D1 registrations by name against the send list.
  • Sending rules (from the onboarding emails plan): no more than one email a day per student, counting the app's own emails; send between 8am and 8pm Central; stop a student's series the moment they hit the goal, reply, or unsubscribe. D2 also needs a decision on the app's 12-hour reminder email (onboarding plan, question 12).

D1

Opened the launch page, never registered

Oct 13 · Oct 16 · Oct 20 · read Oct 27

These students already found Athena and walked away, so we expect most won't come back. If a few short notes that explain what Athena does for them (reads your courses, tells you what to study, coaches you to the answer) bring in some of them, it's worth keeping.

Who
  • Opened the Athena launch page from Hinds Canvas ("You just launched Athena from your LMS") and never registered.
  • Not anyone who has registered. Not anyone who has replied or unsubscribed.
How
  • Three messages, one reason per message, one button each.
  • Leave a random 1 in 5 out and send them nothing, so we can tell what the messages did. Optional; say so and I'll drop it.
  • Don't say or hint that we know they visited ("finish signing up," "still thinking about it"). Each note reads as news about Athena, not a nudge.
A flat result is still useful. It would say the fix belongs on the launch page itself (E5, on hold), not in a follow-up.

D2

Registered, haven't come back

Oct 12 · Oct 15 · Oct 19 · read Oct 26

These students already made an account. If a few short notes show them the one next step, and ask what would make Athena worth opening, some of them come back.

Who
  • About 215 registered students who aren't using Athena (515 registered, about 300 using monthly). We have their emails.
  • Not students using Athena now. Not anyone who replies, unsubscribes, or starts using it mid-series.
How
  • Three plain-text messages from a named person, one button each. Same day spacing as D1.
  • Leave a random 1 in 5 out (about 43 students; about 172 get the series). Optional; say so and I'll drop it.
  • No "free" or price line. They're already in.
  • Optional upgrade: if product can give us each student's last step, swap the step 1 button to match (hasn't connected Canvas: Connect Canvas; connected: Open your Study Space). Not needed to start.
This is a simpler, shorter version of E1 (follow-up emails): one path instead of five groups. If it works, it becomes the first wave of the longer onboarding plan.

Working notes

How this fits the plan

  • E1 to E8 on the project page are on hold while these two run. Nothing was removed.
  • D2 is a smaller E1. E1 sorts the 515 into five groups with different emails; D2 sends one short series to everyone not using Athena. If D2 works, E1's longer plan can build on it.
  • D1 reaches the same people as E2 (remarketing) but by message instead of ads. E2 stays on hold. If D1 can't find a way to reach them, E2 is the other route.
  • D1 and D2 don't overlap. Anyone who registers during D1 leaves D1 and, if they go quiet, becomes a D2 candidate in a later round, not this one.
  • Log the results in reference/traction-channels.md after the Oct 26 and Oct 27 reads, and in the Experiments tab.

Do and don't

  • Short, plain, phone-first. One idea and one button per message.
  • Say what Athena does for them: reads your courses, tells you what to study, coaches you to the answer.
  • Read-only, can't post or submit, disconnect anytime.
  • Write like a coach checking in: calm, warm, never pushy.
  • Say or hint that we know they visited or stopped.
  • Nag, shame, or use "we miss you," "falling behind," or "haven't used."
  • Imply Hinds or an instructor sees an individual student's use, or call Athena part of Canvas or required.
  • Use banned words (unlock, leverage, empower…), "AI-powered," or invented numbers and quotes.
