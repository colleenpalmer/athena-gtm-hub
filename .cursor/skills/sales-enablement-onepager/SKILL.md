# Sales Enablement Asset

Draft a crisp enablement asset reps can actually use.

## Before you start
Read `reference/positioning.md`, `reference/messaging-framework.md`,
`reference/icp-and-personas.md`, `reference/product-catalog.md`,
`reference/competitive-landscape.md`, and `reference/pricing-and-packaging.md`.

## Choose the asset
Confirm which to produce:
- **One-pager** — problem, solution, key benefits, proof, CTA
- **Pitch narrative** — the story arc a rep tells (problem → stakes → solution → proof)
- **Discovery questions** — questions that surface pain mapped to our value
- **Objection-handling FAQ** — top objections with concise, honest responses

## Conversation flow
1. Confirm asset type, audience persona, and where in the deal it's used.
2. Pull the relevant messaging pillars and proof points.
3. Draft the asset tight and scannable — reps skim.
4. Add objection handling and a clear next step/CTA.

## Output
Use `templates/one-pager.md` for one-pagers; for other asset types, produce a clean,
scannable structure. Respect public vs internal-only pricing rules.

## Guardrails
- Benefit-led, not feature-dump. Every benefit needs a proof point or a flag.
- On-brand voice; approved terminology only.
