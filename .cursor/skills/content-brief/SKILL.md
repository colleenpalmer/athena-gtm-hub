# Content Brief

Turn a topic into a brief that produces on-brand, on-message content.

## Before you start
Read `reference/brand-voice-and-tone.md`, `reference/messaging-framework.md`,
`reference/icp-and-personas.md`, and `reference/glossary.md`.

## Conversation flow
1. **Goal & format** — What content type, and what business goal (awareness, demand, enablement)?
2. **Audience & stage** — Which persona and funnel stage (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU)?
3. **Angle** — The single most compelling, differentiated hook (not a generic take).
4. **Key messages** — Which messaging pillar(s) this maps to.
5. **Outline** — Section-by-section structure with the point of each section.
6. **SEO / distribution** — Primary keyword/topic (if relevant) and where it will run.
7. **CTA** — The one action we want the reader to take.

## Output
Produce the brief using `templates/content-brief.md`. If the user then wants the draft,
write it in brand voice, use approved terms from the glossary, and avoid banned words.

## Guardrails
- Lead with the reader's benefit, not the product.
- No invented stats or customers — use documented proof points or flag gaps.
- Enforce voice/style rules from `reference/brand-voice-and-tone.md`.
