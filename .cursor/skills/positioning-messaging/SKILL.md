# Positioning & Messaging Workshop

Run a structured conversation to produce clear positioning and a messaging framework.

## Before you start
Read `reference/company-overview.md`, `reference/icp-and-personas.md`,
`reference/competitive-landscape.md`, `reference/positioning.md`, and
`reference/messaging-framework.md`. Note what's already defined vs placeholder.

## Conversation flow
Work through these one section at a time. Ask focused questions, propose a draft, then refine.

1. **Target customer & problem** — Who is this for (persona/ICP) and what problem/opportunity?
2. **Market category** — What category should buyers evaluate us in? Creating, entering, or reframing?
3. **Competitive alternatives** — What do they use today, including "do nothing"?
4. **Unique attributes → value** — What do we do differently, and what value does each create?
5. **Differentiation** — The 1–3 defensible reasons to choose us, each with proof.
6. **Positioning statement** — Draft using the template below.
7. **Messaging framework** — Value prop headline, elevator pitch, 3 pillars (message + why it
   matters + proof), and per-persona value props.

## Positioning statement template
> For **<target customer>** who **<need/opportunity>**, **<product/company>** is a
> **<market category>** that **<key benefit / reason to believe>**.
> Unlike **<primary alternative>**, we **<key differentiation>**.

## Output
Produce the full framework using `templates/positioning-messaging.md`.
Then offer to update `reference/positioning.md` and `reference/messaging-framework.md`.

## Guardrails
- Every claim needs a proof point or gets flagged as "needs proof."
- Keep language on-brand per `reference/brand-voice-and-tone.md`.
- Prefer sharp and specific over broad and safe.
