Questions instructors ask

Will it do my students' assignments for them?

No. Athena coaches students toward the answer instead of handing it over. It won't produce submission-ready work, and it pushes practice and recall instead of answers.

What can it see in my course?

Only what the student can already see in Canvas, through the student's own read-only permission. Unpublished quiz questions aren't visible to students, so they aren't visible to Athena. It doesn't access anything that contains other students' data, such as discussions.

Can I hide content from it?

No. If a student can see the content, Athena may be able to read it.

Can I see how my students use it?

Not today. Athena is built for students directly. Usage reporting for schools is still being designed, and we can't promise what it will include or when.

Does it cost my students anything?

No. Hinds covers it. [Confirm with product before sending.]

Is student data used to train AI models?

No. Athena data is never used to train external models.

Do I need to install or change anything in my course?

No. Students set it up themselves in a couple of minutes.
