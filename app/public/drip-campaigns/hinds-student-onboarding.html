2 · From reading athena-poc's PostHog instrumentation

What PostHog told us (and what it changes)

1. There are two ways to register, and they start at different stages

  • Path A: "My school pays for Athena." The student logs in to Canvas before the account exists. When institution_signup fires, they're registered, connected, and sponsored all at once. They skip Stage 1.
  • Path B: standard sign-up (paid plan / trial) by a Hinds student. They're registered but not connected or sponsored. They start at Stage 1.

2. Drop-off inside the sponsored sign-up can't be reached by email

In Path A, nothing is saved until the student confirms an email and password. Anyone who quits partway has no account and no email we can use. We can measure this in PostHog ($pageview of /register?institution=…). Fixing it is a product or Hinds-comms job, not a drip job.

Students who fail verification are sent back to /register and "can start a normal paid plan," which puts them into Path B.

3. Connecting Canvas ≠ getting Hinds sponsorship

A Path B student who connects Canvas from Settings stays on the paid plan until they run "Verify with Canvas" (Settings → Billing). That one flow does both: it connects Canvas and grants sponsorship. So Stage 1's CTA is Verify with Canvas, and Stage 1 should end when the student is sponsored. PostHog can't see sponsorship today (subscription_status is Stripe-only, so Hinds students show none).

4. The app already emails new students

onboardingReminder ("Let's get to know each other 😊") goes out 12 hours after a student's onboarding chat is created if they haven't replied. There are also chat/quiz suggestion, due-date, and streak emails, plus a "reconnect Canvas" email for sponsorships that fail re-verification. All go through Resend, which already reports engagement_email_* events back to PostHog (open tracking is off). Our Stages 2–3 overlap with these.

3 · Context

Why this drip, why first

Hinds Community College is offering Athena to all of its students this fall. It's our first institution-sponsored customer and the proof point we lean on when recruiting the 10–15 Fall 2026 pilots. Some Hinds students register and then drop off before they get any value.

Sponsorships are billed per monthly active user. A registered student who never activates is a student Hinds isn't getting value from, and one we can't point to when we tell the Hinds story. Getting onboarding right here matters more than any other lifecycle email right now. (Decision logged 2026-09-29 in decisions/DECISION-LOG.md.)

4 · Who and what

Audience & goal

Audience

  • Who: Hinds CC students who have an Athena account and haven't activated yet (Path A or Path B, see §2).
  • Access: Free to the student; Hinds covers it. Sponsorship comes from a live Canvas enrollment at Hinds, re-checked weekly. A Hinds email address never grants it; it only shows a "your school may cover Athena" banner.
  • Not the D2C ICP. Community college students are a broader group than our core D2C persona (18–22, pre-professional): more part-time, working, and returning students. Keep copy plain, short, and phone-first. Don't make it feel too collegiate.
  • Out of scope: non-Hinds registrants, D2C trial users, and people who quit the sponsored sign-up before an account exists.

Goal

One metric that matters: % of Hinds registrants who activate within 14 days of registering.

Activation = the first of these (PostHog event in brackets):

  • Sending a message / having a chat (chat_message_sent; there's no separate "chat" event, so these count as one)
  • Creating a quiz or flashcards (quiz_question_viewed; no creation event exists, see §8)
  • Starting a tutoring session (learning_session_started)

9 · Guardrails for the system

Sending rules

  • Max 1 email per day per student, including the app's own emails. Skip our send if engagement_email_sent fired for them in the last 24 hours.
  • Send window: 8am–8pm Central (Hinds is in Mississippi). Hold anything triggered outside the window. (timezone is on the person if we want to use it.)
  • Worst case: a student who stalls at every stage gets 9 emails over about 2–3 weeks. Most will get fewer. Path A students get at most 6.
  • Re-check before every send with the most reliable signal available (server-set person props first).
  • Canvas disconnect: PostHog can't see disconnects today, so there's no pause rule for now. The app already emails sponsored students whose re-verification fails. Revisit after eng ask #3
  • Backfill at launch: Hinds students who already have accounts and are stuck get placed in their current stage and receive that stage's first email once. No blast of the full sequence. (Recommended 2026-10-05: count backfilled students separately from the 14-day activation metric. A student caught up weeks after registering isn't a fair test of "activates within 14 days.")
  • Unsubscribe link in every email; honor it across all stages. Decide whether we also respect email_consent_recommendations like the app's emails do.

12 · Voice and claims

Copy guardrails

Do

  • Sound like a coach checking in: warm, short, one CTA per email.
  • Say "free for Hinds students" / "Hinds is covering your access" (factual; confirm wording with Hinds).
  • In Stage 1, be clear that verifying with the Hinds Canvas login is what makes it free.
  • Lead with the benefit; use approved terms: Study Spaces, study coach, walk in ready, no wasted effort.
  • If FERPA comes up: "Hinds has designated Instructure as a school official under FERPA."

Don't

  • "Last chance," urgency, or guilt. Students should never feel nagged or judged.
  • Suggest a Hinds email address is enough to get free access. It isn't; Canvas verification is.
  • Imply Athena is required for coursework, part of Canvas, or endorsed by Hinds beyond sponsoring it.
  • Invent proof: no efficacy stats or student quotes (none approved yet).
  • Lead with "AI-powered," say "FERPA compliant," or use banned words (reference/brand-voice-and-tone.md).
