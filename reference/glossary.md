# Glossary

> Shared vocabulary so we (and the AI) use terms consistently. Includes product terms,
> internal shorthand, and how we translate industry jargon for customers.
> Sourced from `docs/`.

## Product & brand terms
- **Athena** — Our AI study coach; the student-facing masterbrand. *by Instructure*.
- **Study Spaces** — The feature name for Athena's Canvas-native course context (how it
  auto-reads your real courses, materials, and deadlines).
- **Coach-don't-tell** — Our method: guide the student to the answer instead of handing
  it over; won't produce submission-ready work.
- **"The tutor that knows you best"** — The masterbrand idea / durable brand promise.
- **"Knows your class *and* knows you"** — Shorthand for the precision intersection
  (LMS-native context + personal mastery model).
- **Warm expert coach** — The brand archetype/voice (not a peer study-buddy).
- **Applaud mastery, not attendance** — Celebration discipline: reward demonstrated
  competence, never activity or logins.

## Category / industry terms
- **AI study coach** — Our category. We coach toward mastery; we are not an answer
  engine or homework-doer.
- **LMS-native** — Connected directly to the learning management system (Canvas), so
  context is automatic (no upload/config).
- **Mastery model / gap-tracking** — Tracking what a student has mastered vs. where
  they're shaky, using active recall, mastery tracking, and ZPD scaffolding.
- **ZPD (Zone of Proximal Development)** — The learning-science principle behind
  scaffolding a student just beyond what they can already do. Institutional/team register
  only — don't lecture students with it.
- **Student-authorized access** — Our access model: the student authorizes read-only
  access to their own Canvas view via OAuth; we respect access that already exists rather
  than granting new access.

## People / audience terms
- **Students** — The working audience word (bullseye: ~19-year-old undergrad). Prefer
  over "learner" in student-facing copy, though the house glossary may say "learner."
- **Institution / sponsor** — A college, university, or department (success center,
  advising office, Canvas admin) evaluating or paying for Athena on behalf of students.

## Acronyms
- **LMS** — Learning Management System (Canvas is one). Spell out on first use in
  external/student copy.
- **OAuth / OAuth2** — The standard, secure authorization flow Athena uses to connect
  to a student's Canvas (not a personal access token).
- **FERPA** — Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act. Say "Instructure is designated a
  school official under FERPA" (sponsored institutions only). **Never** say "FERPA
  compliant/certified."
- **LTI** — Learning Tools Interoperability. Note: Athena has **no LTI install**.
- **IgniteAI** — Instructure's AI; use "powered by IgniteAI" in the **institutional register
  only**.
- **D2C** — Direct-to-consumer (our primary, student-facing motion).
- **JTBD** — Jobs To Be Done.

## Internal shorthand (don't use externally)
- **Foundry / Instructure Foundry** — The Instructure program Athena launched under
  (Athena is its first project). Fine for context; not a student-facing selling point.
- **Register split** — Internal principle: students hear results; institutions hear the
  learning science and safeguards. Don't reference the concept in external copy.
- **Adoption numbers** — Internal-only (e.g. current connected-student counts). Never
  publish externally.

## Words to avoid (see brand-voice-and-tone.md)
- **Banned (house):** harness, unlock, leverage, empower, future-proof → use "future-ready".
- **Avoid (playbook):** crush it, grind, actually, falling behind, as I mentioned, let's see
  if, that's not quite right, drill me, dominate, hustle, killing it.
