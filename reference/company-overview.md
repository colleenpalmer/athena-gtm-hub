# Company Overview

> Source of truth for what the company is, who it serves, and why it exists.
> Keep this current — skills and chats read this first.
> Sourced from `docs/` (Athena Playbook v1, Creative Brand Brief, Athena & Canvas FAQ).

## What we do (one sentence)
Athena is an AI study coach that knows your courses *and* knows you — so it tells you
what to study next and coaches you until you actually understand it.

## Mission
Give every student the fastest honest path to doing better in the classes that matter —
by coaching them to real understanding, not handing over answers.

## Vision
Be "the tutor that knows you best": a personal learning coach that makes students
measurably more capable, and the safe, sanctioned alternative to the ungoverned AI
tools students already use.

## Company facts
- **Product:** Athena (student-facing brand), *by Instructure*; *powered by IgniteAI* in
  the institutional register only.
- **Parent company:** Instructure (maker of Canvas LMS).
- **Origin:** The first Instructure Foundry project. Announced at InstructureCon on
  July 22 (2026).
- **Availability:** Generally available but not yet formally launched — kept deliberately
  small during validation, with minimal marketing to date.
- **Website:** studywithathena.com
- **Data footprint:** U.S.-based service; data stored and processed in the U.S.,
  encrypted in transit and at rest.
- **Category we play in:** LMS-native, learner-owned AI study coach (on the
  "learn, don't shortcut" side of the AI study-tool category).

## What we sell (high level)
A consumer subscription for students ($5/month after a 7-day free trial) and
institution-sponsored subscriptions that make Athena free to a school's students.
See `reference/product-catalog.md` and `reference/pricing-and-packaging.md`.

## Who we serve (high level)
Primary: traditional undergraduates who want to learn the material (D2C). Growing
second motion: institutions — community colleges and undergraduate universities —
buying seats on behalf of students. See `reference/icp-and-personas.md`.

## Strategic priorities (this year)
1. Validate and improve the product with a small, engaged student base before a
   larger marketing push.
2. Grow the institutional motion — actively recruiting 10–15 institutions to pilot in
   Fall 2026 and shape institutional support features.
3. Add institutional controls (e.g. Canvas-connection opt-in at root, subaccount, and course
   level; usage visibility for sponsored subscriptions) in the spirit of partnership.

## Proof points
- Instructure research released at InstructureCon: **90% of higher-ed students already
  use AI in class at least occasionally.**
- First institution customer: **Hinds Community College**, offering Athena to all its
  students this Fall.
- Privacy posture: read-only Canvas access via standard OAuth2, encrypted OAuth
  credentials at rest (AES-256-GCM), and **no training of external models** on Athena
  data (every AI provider is contractually barred from training on it).
- *Internal-only:* adoption counts (a few hundred students have connected Canvas
  worldwide; highest at a single institution is 16) — do not publish externally.

## What we are NOT
- **Not a spoon-feeding answer machine / homework-doer.** We coach toward mastery
  and won't produce submission-ready work. (We intentionally cede cheat-seekers to
  other tools.)
- **Not a stodgy professor** lecturing that struggle is virtuous.
- **Not a replacement for a human teacher** — we stand alongside the instructor.
- **Not a Canvas sub-brand.** Canvas is a hero feature and campaign angle, not our
  masterbrand or identity frame.
- **Not "AI-powered" as a headline.** AI is the engine, never the badge.
