# Positioning

## Positioning statement
> For **undergraduates who genuinely want to learn the material** and **can't tell where
> their gaps are**, **Athena** is a **personal AI study coach** that **knows your actual
> Canvas courses and knows you — so it tells you the right thing to work on next and
> coaches you until you truly understand it.** Unlike **general chatbots and generic
> study apps**, we **connect securely to your real course content, track what you've
> mastered, and coach you to the answer instead of handing it over.**

**Internal one-liner:** A personal learning coach that knows your actual classes *and*
knows you — the fastest honest path to doing better in the courses you care about.

**Strategic promise (student-facing intent):** *"Do better in the classes that matter —
because you actually get it, not just get past it."*

**Masterbrand idea:** *"The tutor that knows you best."*

## Market category
An independent, learner-owned **AI study coach** that is **LMS-native**. We sit on the
**"learn, don't shortcut"** side of the AI study-tool category — reframing the category
away from answer engines and toward coached mastery.

## Competitive alternatives
- **ChatGPT (free tier)** — used as a homework/explanation crutch; hands over answers
  students don't retain.
- **Quizlet (free)** — generic, manual study sets.
- **Upload-your-syllabus study tools** — require students to paste in a syllabus for context.
- **Do nothing / status quo** — re-reading scattered slides and notes, then cramming.

## Unique attributes
- **LMS-native auto-context (Canvas):** reads real courses, materials, deadlines, and
  exams with zero upload/config. Surfaced today as **Study Spaces**. We are the only
  study tutor that connects directly and securely to Canvas.
- **Rigorous knowledge/mastery model:** active recall, mastery tracking, and ZPD
  scaffolding that pinpoints your gaps ("five concepts for Tuesday, you've nailed three —
  let's drill the two weak ones").
- **Coach-don't-tell method:** guides you to the answer; won't produce submission-ready work.
- **Trust posture:** read-only, student-owned, revocable Canvas access; no training on
  your data; built by the company that runs your LMS.

## Our differentiation (why us)
1. **The precision intersection — knows your class *and* knows you.** No competitor
   combines LMS-native course context with a rigorous personal mastery model.
2. **Only study tutor that connects directly and securely to Canvas** — native context
   without upload friction, plus institutional trust and a data moat competitors can't replicate.
3. **Principled coaching, not answers** — the most *efficient* path to real learning, so
   students do better because they actually understand the material.

## Trends we ride
- Ubiquitous, ungoverned student AI use (90% of higher-ed students already use AI at
  least occasionally) — creating demand for a responsible, safe outlet.
- Institutional anxiety about IP leakage, privacy, and academic integrity from students
  pasting course content into consumer chatbots.

## Register split (load-bearing)
Students hear **results + "no wasted effort"**; institutions hear **the learning science
and safeguards that produce them.** Same truth, two registers. Never pitch grades and
deep understanding with equal weight to the same listener.
