**How to use this.**

We've drafted an email you can send to customers to help communicate about Athena. Here's when to use it:

- A customer reached out about Athena and you need to address key points.

- A customer is upset or wants more details about Athena. Use this as your first response, then use the FAQ for anything else they need.

- You want to reach out early about Athena's positioning before a customer asks.

Initial leanings from students in Athena have been amazing - and we know you have questions! Three quick updates to share with you about your institution, your Canvas instance, and Project: Athena.

**Institutions Opt-in to Athena.** Starting with the August 14 Canvas release, the Athena Canvas connection is something your institution opts into. Until you enable it, students at your institution can't connect Athena to your Canvas instance. You're in control.

**What happens on opt-in:**

- Institutions can enable the Canvas connection to Athena for their students today, free of charge.

- Institutions also have the option to sponsor their students' subscription of Athena. See below.

**Athena isn't GA.** It's an early Foundry project we've kept intentionally small while we learn what actually helps students. Only a few hundred students in North America have connected Canvas so far, and most institutions have zero.
